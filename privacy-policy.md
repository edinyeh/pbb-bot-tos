# Privacy Policy for Prestigious Billionaire Badman
**Last Updated:** 08/12/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Prestigious Billionaire Badman ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with it on Discord.

### 1. Data We Collect
The Bot collects minimal data necessary to function correctly. This may include:
* **Discord User IDs:** Used to track user-specific settings, levels, or permissions.
* **Server (Guild) IDs:** Used to store server-specific configurations and prefix settings.
* **Channel IDs:** Used to send automated messages or store designated channel preferences.
* **Command Usage Data:** Basic logs of commands used to help improve performance and debug errors.

> **Note:** The Bot does **not** permanently store message contents, personal identifying information, or passwords.

### 2. How We Use Data
The collected data is strictly used for:
* Providing and maintaining the core features of the Bot.
* Saving user and server configurations.
* Identifying bugs and improving overall bot performance.

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.

### 3. Data Retention & Deletion
* Data is stored only for as long as necessary to provide the Bot's functionality.
* Server owners or users can request the deletion of their stored data at any time by contacting us directly or by removing the Bot from their server.

### 4. Third-Party Services
The Bot is hosted on bot-hosting.net and interacts directly with the Discord API. We do not transfer your data to external marketing or advertising platforms.

### 5. Contact & Data Requests
If you wish to view, update, or request the deletion of data associated with your Discord account or server, please contact us via Discord at `edinyeh`.
